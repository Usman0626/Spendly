Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you enter into the Service, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and maintain the Service, including logging and displaying your expenses, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and authenticating your account. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your data or account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for functionality such as hosting or email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the Spendly support team.
